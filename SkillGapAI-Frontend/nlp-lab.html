📐 TF-IDF & Cosine Similarity Engine

Mathematical vector space comparison between resume and job description. Uses sublinear term-frequency scaling and L2-normalized vector dot product.

Mathematical Formulations:
• TF-IDF: \(\text{TF-IDF}(t, d) = (1 + \log(\text{TF}(t, d))) \times \left(\log \frac{1 + N}{1 + \text{DF}(t)} + 1\right)\)
• Cosine Similarity: \(\cos(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B}) = \frac{\mathbf{A} \cdot \mathbf{B}}{\|\mathbf{A}\|_2 \times \|\mathbf{B}\|_2} = \frac{\sum A_i B_i}{\sqrt{\sum A_i^2} \sqrt{\sum B_i^2}}\)

💡 Evaluation Notes & Tradeoffs:

• Precision vs. Recall Tradeoff: Rule-based exact matching achieves high precision (0.95+) on canonical keywords but fails to recognize variants or unseen skills. BERT token classification achieves higher recall on novel phrasing.
• Latency vs. Representation Depth: TF-IDF executes in ~1.2 ms with sparse lexical vectors; DistilBERT requires ~45 ms on CPU due to 6-layer self-attention matrix operations.
• Ground Truth Evaluation: Metrics are computed on hold-out annotated samples (backend/data/annotated_skills_dataset.json) following standard Information Extraction evaluation protocols.
